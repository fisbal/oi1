# Google Play listing (English translation)

**App name (30 max)**
Office In 1

**Short description (80 max)**
Write, calculate, present, draw and read PDFs: 7 apps in one.

**Full description (4000 max)**
Office In 1 brings all your office apps together in one app, on phone, tablet and computer.

• In 1 Letter: word processor with 35+ letter templates (administrative, military, official), 20 résumés, one-tap summary and report.
• In 1 Class: spreadsheet with 400+ Excel functions, 42 ready business sheets, world currency converter, Finance, Accounting, Economics and Audit tools, goal seek and scenarios.
• In 1 Stage: presentations with 25 themes, diagrams, animations and animated stickers.
• In 1 Code+: code editor with a smart corrector and built-in courses.
• In 1 ProDFos: PDF reader and tools.
• In 1 Draw: free drawing with finger or pen.
• In 1 Assistant: say what you need and the assistant creates the documents for you in the right apps.

Made to be simple:
• "I want…" bar: type what you want to do, the app opens the right tab and shows the buttons to use.
• A Tutorial tab in every app, with an explanation for each button.
• Convert tab: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, PNG, JPEG and more, plus translation into 35+ languages (the original text is always kept).
• 5 interface languages: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German.
• Pinch zoom, portrait and landscape, fits every screen.

Your documents stay yours: they are saved on your device. AI features are optional and use the service you choose.

Office In 1 is not affiliated with Microsoft, Google or The Document Foundation. The names Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF only describe the compatible file formats.
